Privacy Policy

Model 200 Reverb · Last updated: 7 October 2026

Model 200 Reverb is developed by Past To Future Plugins (“we”, “us”).

What we collect

Nothing. Model 200 Reverb does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We have no analytics, no accounts, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Microphone

When you run Model 200 Reverb as a standalone app, iOS may ask for microphone access so the app can process a live audio input and let you hear the reverb on it. This audio is processed on your device in real time and is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. When the app runs as an Audio Unit (AUv3) plug-in inside another app, audio is handled entirely by that host.

Presets and settings

Your presets and settings are stored only on your device, in storage shared between the app and its plug-in. They are never sent to us.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new “last updated” date.

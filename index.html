Common questions

How do I use it as a plug-in?
Open your host app (GarageBand, AUM, Cubasis, Logic Pro for iPad, Loopy Pro and others), add an Audio Unit effect to a track, and choose Model 200 Reverb from the list. Open the standalone app once after installing so iOS registers the plug-in.

The plug-in doesn't show up in my host.
Launch the Model 200 Reverb app once, then fully close and reopen the host app. If it still doesn't appear, restart the device.

Why does the standalone app ask for the microphone?
So you can hear the reverb on a live input. Audio is processed on your device in real time and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

Are my presets shared between the app and the plug-in?
Yes. Your user registers are shared between the standalone app and the AUv3 plug-in on the same device.
